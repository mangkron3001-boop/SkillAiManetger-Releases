SkillAiManetger Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

SkillAiManetger is an app for Windows and Android that helps you find, save and manage skills for AI coding agents. This policy explains what information the app handles. The app is run by an individual developer, referred to below as "we".

Information we store

You can use the app without an account. If you create an account or sign in, we store:

  • your email address;
  • your display name;
  • your profile picture, if you choose to add one;
  • sign-in records that the authentication service needs to keep you signed in.

If you create an account with an email address and password, your password is stored only in a protected, hashed form. We cannot read it.

Signing in with Google

When you choose "Continue with Google", Google tells us your email address, name and profile picture. We use these only to create or open your account. We do not request access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data.

What stays on your device

The skills you save, their files and the install commands you copy are kept on your own device. They are not uploaded to our servers.

Other services the app contacts

To work, the app sends requests to these services. They receive standard connection details such as your IP address, and the search words or link you enter:

  • skills.sh and GitHub, to search for skills and read their descriptions and releases;
  • GitHub, to check whether a newer version of the app is available;
  • Neon, which hosts the sign-in service, in the Asia Pacific (Singapore) region;
  • Google, when you sign in with Google.

Each of these services has its own privacy policy.

How we use information

We use your account information only to let you sign in and to show your name and picture in the app. We do not sell your information, share it for advertising, or use it to build profiles.

Keeping and deleting information

We keep account information until you ask us to delete it. To delete your account and the information described above, write to the address below and we will remove it.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
